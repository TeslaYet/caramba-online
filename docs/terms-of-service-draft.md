## The service

Carramba Online lets people play a card game together.

- A private room does not require an account.
- Random and ranked games require an account.
- Practice against bots does not change a rating.
- The server decides whether a move is legal. The browser does not.
- Ranked games adjust a rating that starts at 1200. Private, casual, and bot games do not.

The service can be interrupted for maintenance, bugs, or hosting failure. There is no promised uptime.

## Accounts

You are responsible for the email and password on your account. Do not impersonate another person. One person should not operate several accounts to distort a rating or a queue.

You can delete the account from the profile page. The public name becomes “Deleted Player”. Past match rows stay, because deleting them would change other players’ history. Old chat lines keep the nickname that was used when they were sent.

**[LEGAL BUSINESS NAME]** may suspend an account that cheats, harasses, or breaks these terms. Today that is a manual database action, not an automated court. There is no paid appeal desk.

## Behaviour

You may not:

- cheat, automate moves against the server, or exploit a bug instead of reporting it
- harass, threaten, or send illegal content in chat
- manipulate matchmaking or rankings
- abuse bots or script the queue
- attempt to read another player’s hidden cards

Chat is rendered as text. That is a technical limit, not a promise that nobody will type something offensive.

A report button stores the room code, a short excerpt, and your reason. Reports are not a commitment to review every message within a fixed time.

Premium is optional. It removes advertisements. It does not change card rules or the rating formula.

A manual ad-free grant (a friend access entered in the database) is not removed when a Stripe subscription ends.
