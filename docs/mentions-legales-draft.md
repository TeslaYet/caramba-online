**Do not publish this as a finished notice until the brackets are filled. This draft does not invent a publisher.**

French law on confidence in the digital economy (LCEN), article 6, requires a professional website to identify its publisher and its host. Service-Public describes the duty and the information to show: [F31228](https://entreprendre.service-public.fr/vosdroits/F31228). The statute is article 6 of the [21 June 2004 law](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000000801164).

Whether Carramba is already a “professional” site depends on your activity. A site that sells subscriptions or runs a paid advertising network is in that category. A private hobby with no revenue is a question for a professional, not a conclusion this draft can make.
